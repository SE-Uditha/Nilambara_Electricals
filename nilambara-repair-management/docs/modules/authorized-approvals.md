# Authorized Approvals

Open Administration > Authorized Approvals as an administrator or cashier. Pending draft purchases appear automatically. Filter by topic and pending/approved/rejected status, open Review, inspect the purchase and enter a decision comment of at least five characters. Approval finalizes the purchase and records the supplier payable; rejection cancels the draft without receiving goods.

Staff can request payment refunds/full reversals, stock corrections, damage/write-offs and physical counts from the related modules. My requests shows their own submissions. Submission never posts money or stock. Reviewers need existing permissions for the underlying operation, and requesters cannot approve their own manual requests. Cashiers now inherit the former management and stock permissions; stock reviews require those permissions and an active Administrator or Cashier account. Customer quotation decisions remain in Quotations.

Stock counts are rejected if stock changed after the request. Other operations revalidate current balances through transactional services. Failed operations leave the request pending. Repeated decisions cannot double-post or change the prior decision. Requester, reviewer, reasons, comments, timestamps and audit events are retained.

POS payment, return and discount approvals were removed with the POS module on 21 October 2026. The POS retirement migration removes their unused approval columns. See [POS retirement](pos-retirement.md).
