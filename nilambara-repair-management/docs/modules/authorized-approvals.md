# Authorized Approvals

Open Administration > Authorized Approvals (administrator or manager). Pending draft purchases and unverified bank-transfer/cheque POS sales appear automatically, including existing records. Filter by topic and pending/approved/rejected status, open Review, inspect the source document and enter a decision comment of at least five characters.

Purchase approval finalizes the purchase and records its supplier payable; rejection cancels the draft without receiving goods. POS payment approval rechecks stock, records the collection and issues stock; rejection cancels the pending sale without posting money or stock. Customer quotation decisions remain in Quotations.

Staff use Request approval from payment details, stock-item details or POS; My requests shows their own submissions. Supported requests: payment refunds, full payment reversals, POS returns/refunds, stock corrections/damage/write-offs, physical counts and special POS discounts. Nothing posts at submission. Reviewers need the existing permissions for the underlying operation. Requesters cannot approve their own manual requests. Managers retain their current grants; stock approvals require an administrator or a manager separately granted the necessary stock permissions. No role grants are changed by this feature.

Special discount: build the POS cart, request the amount for the intended customer (blank means walk-in), wait for approval, then enter the approved request number and exact discount during checkout. Approval is bound to the requesting cashier, customer, cart quantities, current prices, stock locations and amount. It can be consumed once. A failed checkout rolls back consumption. Changes require a fresh request. Users already authorized to give discounts keep their existing workflow.

Physical counts are rejected at approval if stock moved after the count request. Other stock/financial operations revalidate current state through their existing transactional services. Failed operations leave the request pending. Repeated decisions cannot double-post or change a previous decision. The requester, reviewer, reason, comment, timestamps and audit events are retained. Existing direct operations still enforce their original permission rules; their existing audit history remains in Audit Logs. Decisions made through this page also appear in its history.

Database: additive migration 2026_10_21_150000 creates authorized_approval_requests with InnoDB, utf8mb4, DECIMAL(19,2) amounts, restrictive foreign keys, unique submission/source keys and indexed queue/requester lookups. No existing tables, data, passwords or role grants are removed or overwritten.
