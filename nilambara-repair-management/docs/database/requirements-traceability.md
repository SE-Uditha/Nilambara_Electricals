# Requirements traceability

## Sources and interpretation

The [Business Requirements Document](../../../Repair%20Shop%20Management%20Final.pdf) is the primary source. References below are physical PDF pages 1-20, counting the cover as page 1; page 2 is blank. The Step 2 instruction additionally specifies database entities and integrity rules. Both sources were read completely.

The following labels distinguish their force:

- **BRD mandatory input**: a field explicitly marked with an asterisk and identified as compulsory by the BRD.
- **BRD required capability**: data or behavior the BRD says the system must support; this does not mean every value must exist when an intake or draft is first created.
- **Step 2 requirement**: a database requirement added or clarified by the current instruction.
- **Implementation proposal**: a technical choice that supports the requirements, including normalization, decimal precision, ledger signs, snapshots, and constraints.
- **Conditional/recommended**: scope that the BRD makes optional or recommends separately. A recommendation does not cancel the same feature's mandatory appearance elsewhere.

This document maps requirements to storage responsibility. Exact table/column types, defaults, indexes, foreign keys, and enforced checks belong to the final schema and data dictionary. Relationship contracts are detailed in [relationship-rules.md](relationship-rules.md); unresolved business policies are in [open-decisions.md](open-decisions.md). Steps 3-8 implement employee/account authorization, customer registration, receipt/job, quotation, invoice, payment, and credit workflows. Stock deduction, backup execution, and full financial reporting remain unimplemented.

## Database delivery and administration

All application tables are defined by versioned Laravel migrations in the existing application. Inspect the existing target tables before applying pending migrations; a conflict with an existing table or its data must be reported rather than resolved by deleting it. phpMyAdmin is an administration and inspection interface, not a separate database engine or a substitute for migration-defined application DDL. Do not manually recreate application tables through phpMyAdmin.

The local XAMPP server has been identified as MariaDB 10.4 accessed through Laravel's `mysql` driver. That is the actual local engine; it must not be described as verified MySQL 8. Migration/test execution results are recorded separately from server identification, and MySQL 8 verification remains a distinct unperformed target unless explicitly reported later.

## Exact compulsory input fields

| ID | Source | Exact BRD rule | Database/design interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M01 | Section 4.1, p. 4 | Customer Type: Individual / Company, starred. | `customers.customer_type` must identify one supported type. |
| M02 | Section 4.1, p. 4 | Customer Name OR Company Name, starred. | Require the relevant nonblank name for the selected type. A company contact person is not a substitute for its company name. Separate nullable name columns are a normalization choice, not a requirement to fill both. |
| M03 | Section 4.1, p. 4 | Address, starred. | Customer address is compulsory; an empty or whitespace-only string must not satisfy the rule. |
| M04 | Section 4.1, p. 4 | Mobile Number, starred. | Customer mobile is compulsory. The BRD does not require globally unique customer phone numbers or specify a national validation format. |
| M05 | Section 5.1, p. 5 | Item Name, starred. | Receiving/repair item name is compulsory. |
| M06 | Section 5.1, p. 5 | Item Brand / Model, starred as one entry. | At least the mandatory combined brand/model information must be supplied. Requiring at least one of separate `brand`/`model` values is a documented proposal; the BRD does not clearly say that both are individually compulsory. See OD02. |
| M07 | Section 5.1, p. 5 | Received By: registered receiving person, starred. | Retain a foreign key to a registered employee; a free-text name or unrelated login ID alone does not preserve the registered employee requirement. |

The BRD does not star customer email, alternate phone, contact person, opening balance, notes, receiving fault, accessories, damage, advance, or labour cost. They still require storage support. Technical non-null fields such as IDs, document numbers, source links, timestamps, statuses, and monetary defaults are justified separately by integrity or lifecycle requirements. Nullable completion/inspection/delivery values are appropriate before those events occur. No customer CRUD or Form Request validation is implemented in Step 2.

## Status vocabulary

The BRD's 17 labels are Received; Pending Inspection; Under Inspection; Quotation Pending; Waiting for Customer Approval; Customer Contact Failed; Advance Payment Pending; Waiting for Spare Parts; Under Repair; Price Modification; Re - Assembling; Repair Completed; Ready for Collection; Customer Contact Failed After Ready; Delivered; Cancelled; Unable to Repair. A machine value may use a normalized identifier, but must preserve the label's meaning. Keep original actor/time history when changing the current status.

## Conditional and separately recommended scope

Stock transfers are conditional on need (p. 11). The original expense report is conditional on an expense module (p. 14), although Step 2 now requests expense storage. Supplier purchase print output is conditional (p. 17). Page 18 recommends warranty/notes, repeat repairs, SMS/WhatsApp, collection/overdue alerts, workload reports, low-stock notifications, approval history, audit, and customizable templates. Audit, approvals, low-stock visibility, and template configuration are already required in earlier sections; recommendation wording does not make their core storage optional. External messaging integrations, alert delivery, and a final warranty policy are outside Step 2.

BRD pages 18-20 also cover deployment, manuals/training, ownership, support, licensing, and final acceptance. These are delivery obligations, not extra transaction tables to fabricate. Database tests do not establish that the page 19 end-to-end business acceptance workflow is complete.
