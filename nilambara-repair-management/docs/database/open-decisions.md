# Open decisions and implementation proposals

## How to use this register

The [BRD](../../../Repair%20Shop%20Management%20Final.pdf) is not a complete accounting, stock valuation, or operating-policy specification. This register records ambiguity instead of silently turning it into a final business rule. The Step 2 schema can provide safe storage and conservative restrictions while leaving transaction services, permissions, forms, and final policy for their appropriate later phase.

A **current proposal** explains the database foundation's chosen capacity or restriction. It is not a claim that a business owner approved a final operating policy, or that an unimplemented service enforces it. Read [relationship-rules.md](relationship-rules.md) for integrity boundaries and [requirements-traceability.md](requirements-traceability.md) for the mandatory/conditional distinction.

## Enforcement boundaries

Unique keys, foreign keys and row-level checks enforce only the invariants they express. They cannot by themselves reconcile totals across many payments, ensure a whole-unit rule defined on a separate unit row, prevent concurrent overselling, or guarantee that a history row is written alongside a status change. Eloquent immutability guards protect the normal model path, not arbitrary direct SQL. No triggers or hidden posting observers are introduced merely to imply otherwise.

Closing an open policy should update this register, schema documentation, validation and transaction design, and meaningful tests together. It must not retroactively reinterpret old document snapshots or silently rewrite posted financial/stock history.
