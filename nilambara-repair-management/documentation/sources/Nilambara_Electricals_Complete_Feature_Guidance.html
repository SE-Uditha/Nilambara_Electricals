3. System relationships and posting rules

Repair and financial relationships

Verified table relationships, not a sequence of automatic business actions. Join tables connect the separate paths.

Shared stock, purchase and POS relationships

Verified table relationships, not a sequence of automatic business actions. Join tables connect the separate paths.

  • One customer has repair jobs; each intake creates a receipt/job/card atomically.
  • invoice_jobs links one invoice to multiple same-customer jobs; payment_allocations links multiple receipts to an invoice. Credit is derived from liabilities/allocations and account entries, not a second sale.
  • An advance is money received once. Later allocation is not another cash collection.
  • Supplier purchase finalization creates a payable; GRNs create received stock; purchase drafts do neither.
  • Quoted/requested parts do not deduct stock. Authorized issue and finalized POS consume the shared stock balance.
  • Stock returns/corrections retain original movements. Resalable POS returns retain original cost.

24. Twelve practical workflows

24.1. New customer and repair

Customers > search/register individual/company > New repair receipt > actual intake/advance > Save > receipt/job/card.

24.2. Inspection and quotation decision

Repair Jobs > Assign > technician inspection > Quotations create/issue > record actual customer approval/rejection. No stock posts at quote.

24.3. Repair completion and invoice

Approved work > request/issue parts > actual repair/costs > Repair Completed > Create Invoice > same-customer jobs > Issue > later delivery status.

24.4. Advance and partial payment

Receive advance once > issue invoice > inspect applied advance > receive actual remaining partial amounts > inspect outstanding after each.

24.5. Credit collection

Credit Management > outstanding invoice > Collect > actual payment method/reference > saved allocation > customer statement.

24.6. Purchase and GRN

Supplier > purchase draft > management finalize > receive actual line/location quantities, possibly several deliveries > print GRN > settle supplier.

24.7. Repair parts issue

Technician Part Requests > job/part/location/quantity > issuer checks approval/availability > Issue once > movement history.

24.8. POS sale

POS > scan/search > cart quantity > permitted discount > cash tender/change or pending transfer/cheque > manager verify where needed > receipt.

24.9. Stock return and adjustment

Identify original issue/sale/purchase > choose authorized supported return path > physically verify goods > return/reason > review movement; use damage/count/correction for their actual purpose.

24.10. Dashboard and reports

Dashboard period/date Apply > read cohort/card basis > Business Reports matching filters > inspect incomplete flags > permitted PDF/XLSX/CSV.

24.11. Manual backup

Obtain approved private destination/client configuration first > Backup Management > Create > inspect history > Verify > permitted protected Download; local/Drive states distinct.

24.12. Roles and permissions

Administration > Roles & Permissions/current user > inspect grants > authorized change on eligible other account > verify access; explicit deny and assigned-job policy remain.
