1. Introduction

The system supports appliance/motor repairs, spare-parts retail, shared inventory, suppliers, billing, credit and administrative controls for Nilambara Electricals, 482/A/3, Colombo Road, Kaduwela, Sri Lanka. Currency is LKR; timezone is Asia/Colombo. Intended users are Administrator, Receptionist, Technician, Cashier, Storekeeper and Manager. Browser access, a running PHP application and the existing MySQL-compatible server are required. This manual describes implementation observed on 20 October 2026; owner acceptance is not implied. Training and screenshots use isolated test fixtures, never genuine business records.

2. Getting started

On the existing computer, the operator starts the existing MySQL server and Laravel development server. From the actual application folder, set PHPRC to the repository .tools/php.ini and run php artisan serve --host=127.0.0.1 --port=8000 --no-reload. Open http://127.0.0.1:8000/login (or your operator-provided address). The documentation screenshots used an isolated server on port 8087; do not substitute that temporary port for the shop address. Enter your assigned username and password; use the sidebar and page breadcrumbs to navigate. Logout with the sign-out action. Current configured session lifetime is 120 minutes of inactivity; end-to-end wall-clock expiry was not exercised in this task. Disabled accounts lose access. There is no general self-service account settings/password recovery screen; an administrator manages linked user details/passwords. A forbidden action requires permission review. Do not share logins.

cd D:\xampp\htdocs\Nilambara_Electricals\nilambara-repair-management
$env:PHPRC = (Resolve-Path ..\.tools\php.ini).Path
php artisan serve --host=127.0.0.1 --port=8000 --no-reload

3. Dashboard

Open Dashboard and choose Today, Yesterday, This Week, This Month, This Year or Custom with valid dates, then Apply. Jobs received counts the selected received-date cohort. Active excludes Delivered, Cancelled and Unable to Repair. Pending Inspection, Under Repair, Waiting for Parts, Completed, Ready for Collection and Delivered count the current states of jobs received in that cohort; these are not necessarily jobs whose status changed during the period. Technicians are scoped to assigned jobs. Repair/POS sales sum finalized documents by finalized date. Cash-term sales are not the same as cash actually collected. Payments collected are net receipts after relevant reversals; advances/credit collections are separate categories. Outstanding customer credit is positive cumulative account balance at period end. Low/out stock uses active parts/default locations and reorder thresholds. Dashboard stock valuation currently displays Incomplete; use the dedicated Valuation report for known-cost detail. Recent jobs, invoices and POS sales reflect the corresponding selected cohort/date. Visible cards depend on grants.

4. Customer and company management

Open Customers and search first. To register an individual choose Individual and enter customer name, address, valid mobile and registration date. To register a company choose Company and enter company name, address, mobile and date; contact person/email are optional. Customer code is generated. Save and inspect the profile. Search name/mobile/code/company, use the filters and pagination, then Edit to correct contacts. Activate requires update access; deactivate requires its separate grant and preserves references. Verified pre-system opening balances may be entered only at registration by authorized financial staff; editing cannot rewrite them. Customer profiles link job, invoice/payment and statement histories. In Statement select dates and review brought-forward balance, account movements and closing balance before print/PDF. Required fields, invalid email/phone and unauthorized financial fields return validation errors.

5. Repair receipt management

Open New repair receipt. Select an active customer and receiving employee; enter unique manual Job Number, job type, item name, brand or model and nonnegative labour cost. Record reported fault, accessories, physical condition, visible damage and special notes accurately even where optional. Brand/model cannot both be absent. Enter an advance only when received and select its method; verify method-specific acceptance in the saved payment. Save or Save/Print. The transaction creates receipt, job, initial Received history and job card together; failure rolls the transaction back. Automatic JOB-00001-style reference comes from the database ID and is separate from the immutable staff-entered Job Number. Search receipts by text/date/type/status/receiver. Give the A5 receipt to the customer; subsequent copies use Duplicate. A5 PDF is available on saved documents and creates print/audit history.

6. Repair job management

Open Repair Jobs, Job Status or My Assigned Jobs. Inspect the customer/item/intake and histories before acting. A manager uses Assign to select an active technician; only assigned technicians can inspect/repair that record under the policy. Record inspection findings, repair description and actual costs; selling charge is separate. Choose the actual current status and add notes. An issued quotation must be approved before Under Repair or issue of parts. Completion and delivery fill timestamps when absent; cancellation requires a reason. Printing A6 job cards is a separate permission; preview/PDF the saved card. The 17 statuses below are actual enum values and practical meanings. The implementation does not enforce a complete sequential 17-step transition graph; do not infer an automatic payment/delivery approval from a status label.

7. Quotation management

Open Quotations > Create. Select the correct job, quotation date, valid-until date, proposed repair and labour selling charge. Add at least one part/description line with positive quantity; selected parts supply defaults, while a manual line needs description/unit price. Review decimal line totals, parts total and labour total; Save draft, Preview or Issue. Editable drafts may be revised; issued changes use Create revision so older versions/decisions remain. Preview or download A4/A5 PDF, with duplicate output when appropriate. An authorized staff member records actual Approved/Rejected decision with date/time, contact name and in-person/telephone/email/other method. This records communication, not an electronic signature or verified identity. Approval transfers selling totals without stock/payment. Rejection follows the implemented status handling and retains decision history.

7.1. Actions in Quotation management and decisions

  • Create estimates and draft: Select job; enter date, validity, proposed repair, labour and at least one part/description line; Save draft. Decimal line totals and draft history; no stock deduction.
  • Edit draft: Open editable draft; update allowed estimate lines/dates; Save. Validated estimate revision while draft is editable.
  • Issue quotation: Review customer/job, terms and total; issue the draft for customer decision. Issued snapshot and waiting-approval workflow; no payment.

8. Invoice management

Open Create Invoice. Choose active customer and 1-30 distinct eligible jobs from that same customer; already-billed or ineligible/unapproved work is refused. Review job-wise parts/labour/approved quotation amounts and advances. Enter invoice date, cash/credit terms and optional due date, collector, vehicle, delivery information/date and notes. Preview or save draft, then Issue when reviewed. A draft reserves job links; editing may append jobs but cannot remove original links or silently reprice them. Issue posts the customer liability and allocates eligible advances once. Printing is A5 with marked duplicate and PDF options under policy permissions. Amount due is derived from invoice amount and net allocations; changing terms does not receive money. Invoice delivery metadata is separate from job Delivered status.

9. Payment management

Open Record payment. Select Invoice payment or Advance. For an invoice choose the saved invoice; for an advance select the customer and optional job, not an invoice. Enter actual positive two-decimal amount and payment date. Cash, bank transfer and cheque have their displayed reference/bank/date fields; cheque date belongs only to cheque payments. Receiver is the signed-in active employee unless receive-for-others is granted. Save once and review allocations. Partial payments may repeat as money is received. Multiple payments can settle an invoice; the UI receives against one invoice per entry, so a multi-invoice allocation form is Not Implemented even though allocation services support multiple records. Print/PDF/duplicate the saved payment receipt. Authorized Refund and Accounting reversal are distinct; retain original, provide reason and record an actual refund method where appropriate. Reversals release allocations and affect collection/account history without deleting the original sale.

11. Stock and inventory

Under Stock & Purchasing open Inventory > Register part. Enter unique SKU, name, unit/precision, selling price, location and reorder thresholds; barcode/category/brand/preferred supplier are optional. Register permitted references if needed. Opening quantity posts once; provide opening cost only when known. Edit allowed details later, but unit is locked after stock movements. Stock is received through purchase GRNs. Technicians request parts against accessible jobs; a request neither reserves nor deducts stock. Authorized issuers check approval and availability, then issue; POS and repairs use the same locked balance. Before billing an unissued request can be cancelled or an entire unused resalable issue returned. Partial issue return is Not Implemented. For corrections/damage use signed quantity/location/reason; damaged stock is removed. Physical Count records expected/actual and posts the difference. Check movement history and Low/Out report/dashboard alerts. No negative available stock is permitted.

13. POS system

Open POS. Search SKU/barcode/product/brand/category, or focus the barcode field and scan. Add product, change quantity or remove a line. Review location availability and unit precision. A discount requires its dedicated permission. Cash checkout needs tendered amount; verify change. Sale, total-only payment and stock deduction post atomically. Repeated identical protected retries return the same sale; changed payload cannot reuse the operation key. Bank transfer/cheque creates a pending draft with references; an authorized manager verifies actual receipt before stock/payment posting. A recorded cheque is not claimed cleared. Use POS sales history to open saved sales and print/reprint/download the configured receipt. Authorized Returns/Refunds reference original sale quantities, distinguish resalable/damaged goods and preserve the original.
